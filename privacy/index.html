UltraMLX privacy policy

This policy covers the UltraMLX macOS app and this support site.

Local use

For local chat, UltraMLX sends your requests to its inference service running on your Mac. Your chat input and output, model files, and local inference measurements are handled on your Mac. The macOS file chooser grants UltraMLX access to the folders you select.

Optional dictation uses on-device speech recognition. You can change microphone and speech permissions in macOS System Settings.

Model catalogs and connected services

When you search a model catalog or download a model, the catalog or file hosting provider receives your request. The provider may receive information such as your search terms, network address, and ordinary connection details. Each provider describes its own use and retention of that information. See the Hugging Face privacy policy.

If you choose to configure and use an external connector or a forum relay, the service you select receives the information needed for the action you request. Its terms and privacy policy apply to that information. You can disconnect external services you configure.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the UltraMLX project receives your email address and the information you include. We use this correspondence to respond to you and investigate your issue. We keep it as needed to handle the request and follow up. Email ultramlx@pm.me to ask about your information or request deletion of your support correspondence.

Advertising and website hosting

UltraMLX carries no advertising and has no default app analytics collector. This support site uses no tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this site and can log requests for security and service operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Manage your information

Use Finder to manage the model files in folders you selected. Use macOS System Settings to manage microphone and speech permissions. Contact ultramlx@pm.me for privacy questions about UltraMLX or this site.
