UltraMLX support

UltraMLX requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Start a local chat

  1. Open Models, choose a folder you own, then open Downloads.
  2. Search the Hugging Face catalog for Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-4bit. Download the exact match and wait for Downloaded. The transfer requires an internet connection and about 350 MB of available storage for the files and temporary space.
  3. Wait for the server to become idle, then choose Server and Restart. Wait for Healthy.
  4. Return to Models, choose Use beside the model, then open Chat.

Contact support

Email ultramlx@pm.me. Include your macOS version, your UltraMLX version, and the steps that reproduced the issue. Share only the details needed to help diagnose the problem.
